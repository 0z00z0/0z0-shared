**The bracket button's light-theme colours darken again, this time for the About popup itself.** No
public member changes, and the four colours keep their hues while clearing 4.6:1 wherever the button
sits.

**Patch.** Nothing recompiles, and nothing about `BrandBracketButtonState` or the button's members
moves.

## What 0.9.0 measured, and what it missed

0.9.0's light values cleared 4.5:1 against WinUI's base window (`#f3f3f3`) and its default card fill
(`#fbfbfb`), the two grounds its own guide measured against. `BrandBracketButton` also renders inside
`BrandAboutWindow`, the About popup, whose Mica Alt backdrop is darker still in light theme — around
`#dadada`. Against that ground the four colours measured 3.96 to 3.99:1, short of the 4.5:1 text
floor.

## Darker again, same hue

The chevron, teal, amber and the bracket gradient's lower stop each take a darker shade at the same
hue and saturation, chosen so every one clears 4.6:1 against all three light grounds rather than just
the two the previous release checked:

| Used for | 0.9.0 | 0.9.1 | Window / card / Mica Alt |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Chevron | `#985916` | `#895014` | 5.88 / 6.30 / 4.66 |
| Teal (spinner, caret, `Success`) | `#117568` | `#0f695e` | 5.91 / 6.34 / 4.69 |
| Amber (`Attention`) | `#896120` | `#7b571d` | 5.87 / 6.30 / 4.66 |
| Bracket gradient, lower stop | `#0b718f` | `#0a6681` | 5.86 / 6.28 / 4.65 |

Dark theme and high contrast are unchanged: the palette's own teal, amber, orange and blue in dark,
the system highlight colour in high contrast.

## What has not changed

`BrandBracketButton`'s public members, `BrandBracketButtonState`, `Brand.cs` and
`Themes/BrandResources.xaml` — the button's colours have always lived in its own resources, not the
shared palette. Nothing else in `ZeroZero.Brand.Core` or `ZeroZero.Brand.WinUI` changes; both release
under the same number.
