**A socket that never opened on an encrypted candidate never opens the clear-text retry.**

**Released** 2026-10-12

**Patch.** Nothing a consumer compiles against moves. Takes `ZeroZero.Primitives` 0.8.0,
`ZeroZero.Config` 0.9.0 and `ZeroZero.Controls.WinUI` 0.10.0.

## Fixed

- The live connection ends a candidate on any verdict from opening its socket, as the connection
  check does. A socket that fails or times out on an encrypted candidate is reported as such, never
  read as "no TLS there", and never opens the clear-text retry.

## Changed

- The settings panel's four section headings are the controls package's `SettingsSectionHeader`,
  in the panel's heading colour. They look the same.
- The guide says where a dashboard shows an entity's `Description`.
