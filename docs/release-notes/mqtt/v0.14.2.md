**The settings panel and the log no longer use the word broker, except for the section heading.**

**Released** 2026-10-25

**Patch.** Only text values change; no key, identifier, setting or API moves. Takes
`ZeroZero.Primitives` 0.9.1, `ZeroZero.Config` 0.9.0, `ZeroZero.Controls.WinUI` 0.11.0 and
`MessageFormat` 8.0.0.

## Changed

- The panel texts in English (`Texts.resx`) and Norwegian (`Texts.nb.resx`) say server, server
  address or connection where they said broker, and the section heading `HeadingBroker` stays
  "Broker". The Norwegian loan "brokeren" is gone from the panel.
- Three texts that named MQTT say remote control instead: the line under the master switch, the
  last-command bubble and the port bubble.
- Seven log lines say "the server" where they said "the broker": the outage and refusal reasons, the
  lost-connection line and the refused-publish error.

An application that overrides these keys in its own resources keeps its own text.
